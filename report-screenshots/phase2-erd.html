Relationships (all one-to-many, enforced by foreign keys with cascade rules)

classroom_sections 1 ──< N classroom_users classroom_sections 1 ──< N usage_records classroom_rooms 1 ──< N classroom_users classroom_rooms 1 ──< N usage_records classroom_users 1 ──< N usage_records appliances 1 ──< N usage_records
Invariants enforced by the design: a user holds at most one assigned room · a usage record targets the user's assigned room · appliances with usage history cannot be deleted (HTTP 409) · seeded baseline: 3 buildings × 4 floors × 5 rooms = 60 rooms, 4 sections, 5 appliances.
